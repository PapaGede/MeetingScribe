# MeetingScribe

Live transcription of Zoom, FaceTime and other meetings on your Mac. Free, with no account. Speech recognition (Whisper) and AI summaries run on your Mac, so audio never leaves it.

## Download

**[⬇︎ Download the latest version](https://github.com/PapaGede/MeetingScribe/releases/latest)**, then click **MeetingScribe.zip** under *Assets*.

Works on macOS 13.3 or newer, on Apple Silicon and Intel Macs.

## Install (one time)

1. Double-click the zip, then drag **MeetingScribe** into your **Applications** folder.
2. Double-click it. macOS says it "can't be opened". Click **Done**.
3. Open **System Settings › Privacy & Security**, scroll down, and click **Open Anyway** next to MeetingScribe.
4. Inside the app, download the speech model when asked (one time).

## Updates

MeetingScribe updates itself. When a new version is out, it shows **"A new version is available"**. Click **Install & Restart**.
